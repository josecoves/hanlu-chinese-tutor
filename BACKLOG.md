# Hanlu product backlog

## AI-assisted answer review

- Replace the current queued review workflow with an optional real-time AI
  second opinion.
- The AI should judge the grammar point being practiced separately from
  vocabulary, register, spelling, and naturalness.
- Return a short verdict, explain meaningful differences, and never interrupt
  the next-card flow with a mandatory dialog.

## Personalized weekly coaching

- Compile English-to-Chinese grammar attempts, especially incorrect answers,
  manual overrides, and queued reviews.
- Generate an optional weekly feedback report that identifies recurring grammar
  and vocabulary gaps.
- Suggest focused lessons and new exercises from those gaps without
  automatically changing lesson status.
- Keep the report grounded in the learner's saved attempts and make every
  recommendation dismissible.

## Curriculum quality

- Continue auditing practice pools so an exercise primarily tests its stated
  lesson and does not require later grammar as the only model answer.
- When later grammar or vocabulary appears incidentally, expose it as help and
  label it rather than grading the learner on an unintroduced structure.
- Review queued answer disputes in batches and feed confirmed corrections back
  into the deterministic grader and curated exercise bank.
