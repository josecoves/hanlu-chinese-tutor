# Tatoeba sentence attribution

Contextual Mandarin/English sentence pairs in `context_sentences.json` are derived from
[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/) and are included under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

The local importer records these entries with source `Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0)`.
Four uncovered Family & People examples are original authored fallbacks and are marked
separately in the data.
